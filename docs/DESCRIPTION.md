## One paragraph (about 120 words)

Fresing Render turns your CAD model into renders and animations – fast enough for engineers to make them themselves, and a ready-made start for animators. Import, sorting, materials and motion take a few clicks instead of hours of set-up, step by step and in plain words. Every frame comes from your own CAD: true shape, true sizes, true motion, nothing guessed. Sales teams can show a client how the machine looks and moves, early and convincingly, straight from the engineering model. Many animators worry about what AI means for their work; Fresing Render is built on the opposite idea. It takes over the repetitive set-up, so animators start from a working scene and spend their time on light, camera, timing and story. It stays a normal Blender scene, so skilled animators can take it as far as they like. Free and open source.

## The four points

- **Easy for engineers.** Step by step, in plain words.
- **Fast.** Import, sorting, materials and motion in a few clicks instead of hours of set-up.
- **No limits for animators.** It stays a normal Blender scene: light, camera, effects and fine motion are all yours to push further.
- **Your real machine.** Every frame comes from your own CAD – true shape, true sizes, true motion. Nothing is guessed.
