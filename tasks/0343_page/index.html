Companies pay, private use stays free

Task 0343 · findings only · 25 Nov 2026. Not legal or tax advice. ★ = recommended (always the first choice).

In short
  • Allowed: the code stays GPL; Fresing's own pictures, HDRIs, material libraries and projects can have their own licence – in their own folder, and the app must run without them.
  • The catch: almost all of today's looks (87 materials, 25 finishes, 5 light looks, weather, surroundings, people, demo, lessons) are program code, released under the GPL in all 232 versions up to 0.2.248. Those versions stay free for everyone, for ever. The paid part can only be new things: your own HDRIs and 360° photos, hand-made finish and environment libraries, learning projects.
  • No lock, no online check: companies pay by invoice and get a licence file the app only shows.
  • Price proposal: €129 once per company user, €59 for a freelancer (KeyShot: $1,299–1,599 a year; Enscape: $460–585 a year; Rhino: $995 once).
  • Name: unregistered, "Fresing" is barely protected. Register in the Benelux before the first sale (2 classes: €271).

Who pays, in plain words

Free: a person's private use, not for paid work · pupils, students, teachers and researchers, for learning, teaching and research · registered non-profits (see choice 5).
Paid: every company, freelancers included, and any paid work for a client.
Always the user's: renders, videos, pictures and web 3D pages made with the assets. You may sell them and need not credit Fresing.
Not allowed: passing the assets on by themselves. Sending a client a project file with the assets inside is fine.
A copy of the code: allowed under the GPL, but without the assets folder and without the Fresing name and logo.

Draft licence (a lawyer may word it)

Fresing Assets Licence 1.0
1. What it covers. The files in the folder fresing_assets and any pack named "Fresing Assets": pictures, HDRIs, 360° photos, materials, environments and projects, © Fresing. It does not cover the Fresing Render software (GPL-3.0-or-later) or other people's files (they have their own licences, listed in the pack).
2. Free use – no payment needed: (a) a person for private use, not for paid work and not for a business; (b) pupils, students, teachers and researchers of a school, university or training centre, for learning, teaching and research, not for paid work for a client; (c) a registered non-profit organisation, for its own activities that it does not sell.
3. Company use – all other use, by or for a business (a one-person business or freelancer included) or for paid work, needs a paid licence: one per user, or the number of users on the invoice.
4. You may use the assets in your projects. Everything you make with them is yours, also to sell, without credit. You may send a project with the assets inside to a client or partner, so they can open and render that project.
5. You may not sell, give or publish the assets by themselves or in another library, pack or product, or remove this licence or the file that names the licensee.
6. Copies of the software. The GPL lets anyone copy and change Fresing Render. This licence does not let a copy given to others include the assets or use the Fresing name and logos.
7. Proof. Your invoice and licence file (company, number of users, invoice number). There is no online check.
8. No warranty. Fresing's liability is limited to the price paid. Dutch law applies.
9. Ending. The licence ends if you break it and do not put it right within 30 days of being told.
