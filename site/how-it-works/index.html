// How it works

From your CAD model to a finished render – in minutes

Fresing Render does the set-up that used to take hours: it reads the model, sorts the parts, gives them materials, makes them move and builds the scene. Engineers and sales teams stop there with a finished picture or film. Animators carry on in Blender with everything already in place.

// The steps

The steps, in order

  1. 01

    Import

    Open the STEP file from Inventor, Fusion or any CAD. Thousands of parts are read on all processor cores; a new revision later keeps all your work.

    User guide: Import

  2. 02

    Sort and give materials

    One click sorts the parts into groups – bolts by their DIN/ISO names, steel, aluminium, paint, rubber, glass – each with its material. Change any of them with a click.

    User guide: Looks

  3. 03

    Motion

    Click a round face to make a joint, let cylinders follow their arm, run your G-code on a CNC mill. Poses and exploded views on top.

    User guide: Motion

  4. 04

    Surroundings and camera

    Put the machine in a studio, a hall, on a quay or at sea, pick a light look and the camera's view.

    User guide: Surroundings · Camera

  5. 05

    Render, video or web 3D

    A still in print size, a turntable, a finished video with captions and music, or a 3D page anyone can turn in a web browser. The render queue does it while you keep working.

    First render · Video · Web 3D model · Render queue

  6. 06

    Open in Blender

    It stays a normal Blender scene. One click shows all of Blender: light, camera, effects and fine motion are yours to push further.

    User guide: All of Blender

// For animators

Speed the animator up – not replace them

Many animators worry about what AI means for their work. Fresing Render is built on the opposite idea: speed the animator up, not replace them. It takes over the repetitive set-up, so you start from a working scene and spend your time where an animation is won – light, camera, timing, story. You deliver faster and stay competitive, and the result still carries your skill.

Every frame comes from the real CAD: true shape, true sizes, true motion. Nothing is guessed – and it stays a normal Blender scene, so you can take it as far as you like.
