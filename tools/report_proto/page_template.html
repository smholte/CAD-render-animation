Learning from users – "Send a report" now, opt-in statistics later

Task 0288 · study, findings only · 23 Nov 2026 · nothing in the app is changed. The report text, the summary and the trace on this page are made by the prototype on the branch (tools/report_proto/report_proto.py) from made-up files. The full report with every source: tasks/0288-study-user-feedback-data.md.

In short
  • Step 1 – Send a report: one window (Help menu, Fresing menu, after an error, at the end of a PC check) shows the whole report in plain text. The user can untick parts, then Copy, Save as file or E-mail it with his own mail program. The app sends nothing itself. A public bug goes to GitHub as today, with the version already filled in.
  • Step 2 – weekly statistics: off unless the user says yes. The app asks with two equal buttons and no box ticked in advance. A summary of about 1 KB goes out once a week; this one is {{SUMMARY_BYTES}} bytes. It carries a random id the user can reset, and Show what was sent and Delete my data are in Settings. A company can switch it off for everyone in its .frsetup.
  • Where it goes: my suggestion is a Supabase project in Frankfurt behind the app's own address (stats.<Fresing domain>). It costs $0 for a pilot and $25 a month for real use, at any of 100 / 1,000 / 10,000 users. The alternative is a Hetzner server in Germany at about €6 a month, which needs more upkeep. Raw rows are kept 90 days, then only totals with no id.
  • Never collected: part, file, folder, project, workspace and company names; paths; pictures; models; G-code; user and computer names; times of day.
  • This is not legal advice. The questions for a privacy advisor are in section E of the report.

In the first-start set-up (0276), at the end of step 3 "Your PC"

Help make Fresing Render faster?

If you say yes, the app sends a small summary to Fresing once a week: the app's version, the kind of PC (for example "NVIDIA RTX 40-series, 64 GB"), how often you used each function, how long imports and renders took, and where the app ran into an error. Never your models, pictures, file or part names, your name or your company's name. It is stored in the EU, only Fresing can see it, and it is never sold.

It stays off unless you say yes. You can see every summary, stop at any time and delete what was sent: Fresing > Settings… > My usage.
