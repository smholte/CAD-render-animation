// What it does

What it does

  1. 01

    Drop in a STEP file

    Assemblies with thousands of parts, read on all processor cores; a new revision of the model keeps all your work.

  2. 02

    Parts sorted and given materials

    Bolts by their DIN/ISO names, steel, aluminium, paint in RAL colours, rubber and glass – picked for you, changed with a click.

  3. 03

    Motion

    Rotating and sliding joints, hydraulic cylinders that follow their arm, mechanisms such as scissor lifts and slider-cranks.

  4. 04

    CNC milling

    Your G-code drives the machine; material disappears where the tool cuts, chips fly and the air blast blows them away.

  5. 05

    Surroundings

    Open sea, a vessel deck, a quay, factory and CNC halls, or a clean studio with ready-made light looks.

  6. 06

    Stills, turntables and finished videos

    Print sizes up to posters, one-click turntables, videos with title and end cards, captions, labels and music.

  7. 07

    Render queue

    Line up pictures and videos and let them render on the graphics card while you keep working.

  8. 08

    Updates itself

    A new version is offered in the app: one click and it installs.

// Built on Blender

Built on Blender

Fresing Render is built on Blender, the free and open-source 3D software. Every picture and video is rendered with Blender's Cycles engine: real light, real reflections and real shadows, the same kind of light simulation used for films and product photos.

You don't need to know Blender. The Fresing Render installer downloads Blender {{blender}} LTS, checks it and sets it up as a standalone app: its own Fresing Render icon on your Desktop, its own settings and a side panel made for machines, separate from any other Blender on your PC. Updates come from inside the app.

Blender is a trademark of the Blender Foundation. Fresing Render is not made or endorsed by the Blender Foundation.

// Install

Install in 3 steps

  1. Download {{asset_name}}.
  2. Unzip it: right-click the file, Extract All…, then Extract.
  3. Double-click Install.bat in the unzipped folder. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. When it is done, start Fresing Render from the Desktop.

The installer downloads Blender {{blender}} (about 400 MB) and keeps it separate from any other Blender on your PC.
