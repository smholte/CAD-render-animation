What it does

  • Drop in a STEP file. Assemblies with thousands of parts, read on all processor cores; a new revision of the model keeps all your work.
  • Parts sorted and given materials. Bolts by their DIN/ISO names, steel, aluminium, paint in RAL colours, rubber and glass – picked for you, changed with a click.
  • Motion. Rotating and sliding joints, hydraulic cylinders that follow their arm, mechanisms such as scissor lifts and slider-cranks.
  • CNC milling. Your G-code drives the machine; material disappears where the tool cuts, chips fly and the air blast blows them away.
  • Surroundings. Open sea, a vessel deck, a quay, factory and CNC halls, or a clean studio with ready-made light looks.
  • Stills, turntables and finished videos. Print sizes up to posters, one-click turntables, videos with title and end cards, captions, labels and music.
  • Render queue. Line up pictures and videos and let them render on the graphics card while you keep working.
  • Updates itself. A new version is offered in the app: one click and it installs.

Install in 3 steps

  1. Download {{asset_name}}.
  2. Unzip it: right-click the file, Extract All…, then Extract.
  3. Double-click Install.bat in the unzipped folder. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. When it is done, start Fresing Render from the Desktop.

The installer downloads Blender {{blender}} (about 400 MB) and keeps it separate from any other Blender on your PC.
